## 4. Core Design Decisions & Rationale

1. **Scene-as-Page Spatial Narrative (`taste.md` Section 1.5)**:
   - Rather than placing the 4 generated images inside cards or split-screen boxes, the entire viewport acts as a continuous camera moving from predawn mountain mist (`hero-01` / `story-01`) toward the warm amber roastery cabin window (`story-02`) and finally onto the wooden table with the 200g kraft coffee bag and steaming mug (`story-03`).
2. **Hero Discipline (`#top`)**:
   - The hero displays only `hero-01.webp`, the restrained top-left wordmark `半山烘焙`, one headline sentence (`豆子的产地和处理法，自己会说话。`), and one primary action (`走进半山烘焙间`) positioned in the dark left-third negative space (`titleSafeThird`). No navigation bar, badges, bean cards, or prices appear in the hero.
3. **Open Typographic Ledgers Instead of Card Grids**:
   - All three beans (`云雾水洗 ¥68 / 200g`, `火塘日晒 ¥58 / 200g`, `山脊蜜处理 ¥72 / 200g`) and subscription options (`按月寄送，每期 1 袋 200g`、`¥62 / 月起`、`¥118 / 月`、`每 2 周 / 每月 / 每 6 周`) are set as open typographic ledgers using left amber hairlines and horizontal hairlines directly over the dark left third of the scene, never collapsing into boxed card grids.
4. **Zero External Runtime Dependencies**:
   - Detected locally installed variable CJK fonts (`Noto Serif SC` and `Noto Sans SC`) and implemented the scroll-driven camera controller, natural atmosphere layers (mist `44s`/`54s`, window light breathing `8s`, cup steam `38s`, motes `8.6s–13.8s`, SVG film grain `0.14`), and interactive subscription customizer in vanilla CSS/JS, guaranteeing zero network/CDN failures during offline or `file://` execution.

  - Removed 4-sided borders and border-radius from `.scene-act` and `.menu-row`, replacing them with open typographic ledgers anchored by a single left amber hairline (`border-left: 1px solid`) and horizontal hairlines.
